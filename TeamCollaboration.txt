What is TeamCollobration?

Descrption:
Team collaboration is the process of working together to achieve a common goal.
It involves sharing ideas, responsibilities, and communication among team members.
It helps improve productivity, creativity, and project success.

Flow Representation :

Communication
Team members share ideas, updates, and information clearly.
===================
Task Assignment
Work is divided among team members based on skills.
==================
Planning
Setting goals, deadlines, and strategies for the project.
=====================
Execution
Team members work on their assigned tasks.
========================
Coordination
Ensuring all tasks are aligned and team members support each other.
============================
Review
Checking progress and giving feedback for improvement.
==============================
Completion
Finishing the project and delivering the final result.
