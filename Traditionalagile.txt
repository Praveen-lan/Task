Difference between Traditional vs Agile?

Descrption:
Traditional model follows a fixed, step-by-step process where changes are hard to make after planning.

Agile model is flexible, works in small cycles, and easily adapts to changes based on feedback.
===========

Flow Representation:

Traditional:

Requirement Gathering
Design
Development
Testing
Deployment

Agile:

Requirement Gathering
Sprint Planning
Design
Development
Testing
Review
Deployment & Iteration
