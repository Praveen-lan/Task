What is Jira?

Descrption:
========
Jira is a project management and issue-tracking tool used in software development.
It helps teams plan, track, and manage tasks and bugs efficiently.
It is widely used in Agile methodologies like Scrum and Kanban.

============
Flow Representation:
============
Create Issue
A task, bug, or story is created in Jira.
===================


Backlog
All issues are stored and prioritized for future work.
=============================


Sprint Planning
Selected issues are moved into a sprint for development.
==============================


In Progress
Team members start working on assigned tasks.
========================


Testing
Completed tasks are tested for quality and correctness.
===================


Review
Work is reviewed and feedback is given.
=========================


Done
Final tasks are completed and marked as finished.
=========================
