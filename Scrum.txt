what is Scrum?

Descrption:
Scrum is an Agile framework used to manage and complete complex projects.
It divides work into small time periods called sprints for faster delivery.
It focuses on teamwork, regular feedback, and continuous improvement.

flow Representation:
=============

Product Backlog
List of all features, tasks, and requirements for the project.
===================


Sprint Planning
Selecting tasks from the backlog to work on in the sprint.
============================


Sprint (Execution)
Team works on tasks during a fixed time period (usually 2–4 weeks).
=========================


Daily Scrum (Meeting)
Short daily meeting to discuss progress and issues.
=====================


Development & Testing
Building the product and checking for errors.
=====================


Sprint Review
Showing completed work to stakeholders and getting feedback.
==================


Sprint Retrospective
Discussing what went well and what can be improved for next sprint.
================
